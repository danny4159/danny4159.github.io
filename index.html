ABOUT ME

Building reliable AI
for medical imaging.

I develop deep learning methods for medical image registration and synthesis, with a focus on learning from imperfectly aligned or corrupted data. My research explores reference-augmented generation, self-supervised learning, and medically meaningful image analysis.
